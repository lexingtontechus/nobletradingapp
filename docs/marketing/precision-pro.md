## A trading agent that works the markets so you don't have to

Precision Pro is Noble Trading's flagship tier: the full Talaria client plugin experience, running beside your chat inside the Hermes desktop app. Talaria is your trading agent — it watches 20 curated markets around the clock, qualifies high-confidence signals, and delivers each one with the levels that matter: entry, stop-loss, and take-profit. ## What you get with Precision Pro

- **Twenty curated symbols** — double the 10 offered on Signal Scout — spanning commodities, forex, crypto, and equities.

- **Qualified signals only** — the engine gates for high-confidence setups; the hot-signals banner shows what's actionable right now, ranked by conviction.

### Agent-grade analytics — Precision Pro exclusive

- **Simulated paper portfolio** with a daily equity curve, realized PnL, and R-multiple per trade — a transparent record of what the agent would have traded, with no real money at risk.
- **Portfolio analytics**: Sharpe, Sortino, Calmar, max drawdown, annualized volatility, and profit factor — the risk-adjusted tear-sheet serious traders ask for.
- **Paper vs. equal-weight benchmark** — the daily question answered plainly: *is the strategy beating the benchmark?*
- **30-day signal health scoreboard** per symbol — win rate, calibration bias, and profit factor — so you can see which markets carry the edge and which deserve caution.
- **Signal science on demand** — Markov P(up in 3) probabilities, brick-pattern analysis, and a sizing what-if calculator that follows the symbol you're examining.
