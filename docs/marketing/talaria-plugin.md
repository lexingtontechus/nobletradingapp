# Talaria — Live trading signals, docked beside your chat

Talaria is the Noble Trading signal dashboard that lives inside the Hermes desktop app — the same window you already use for chat, agents, and work. No separate platform to open, no extra terminal to babysit: connect your subscription once and the dashboard unlocks. It shows you what the signal engine sees right now — price structure, market regime, signal health, and a simulated portfolio — refreshed automatically while you work.

When a qualified signal fires, it comes to you. A status-bar chip, a docked side pane, and a single persistent alert — local time and market regime in plain words — keep the signal stream visible beside your conversation, whether or not the dashboard is open.

## What you get

- **Live signal dashboard.** A dedicated dashboard view with per-symbol renko charts — price action rebuilt as fixed-size bricks, so only real moves count — and full entry, stop-loss, and take-profit prices on every signal. No truncated digits, no guessing the numbers.
- **Market-regime labels.** Every signal carries a plain-language regime tag — bull, bear, high or low volatility — so you know the engine's read on the market before you look at the numbers.
- **Hot-signals banner.** Colored chips of the most recent qualified signals, ranked by the engine's current conviction, each with a friendly regime tag. Stale signals drop off after ten minutes — this is always the "what's actionable right now" view.
- **Signal health scoreboard.** Per-symbol performance over the last 30 days: win rate, calibration bias (does the engine win as often as it predicts?), and profit factor — so you can see which symbols' signals deserve your attention.
- **Signal science, per symbol.** Pick any symbol and get the full read: a brick-pattern analysis of the recent price shape, a probability model of the odds the next move is up, and a sizing what-if showing the suggested position size for the latest signal — the same math the engine uses, shown transparently.
- **Signals beside your chat.** A dockable status-bar chip and side-pane widget keep new signals visible next to your chat session, and one persistent in-app alert updates with each new qualified signal — local time and regime included, no notification spam.
- **Paper portfolio (Precision Pro).** A simulated trade book — no real money — with a daily equity curve, realized profit measured in units of risk per trade, and a risk-adjusted scorecard: Sharpe, Sortino, Calmar, max drawdown, volatility, and profit factor.
- **Benchmark honesty.** Paper results are compared daily against an equal-weight benchmark, answering one plain question: is the strategy actually beating just trading everything equally?
- **Daily digest.** A daily rundown of signal and performance highlights, delivered to wherever you've connected Hermes.

## Who it's for

Talaria is for traders who want the engine's read without adding another screen to their workflow. If you live in the Hermes desktop app and trade crypto, forex, or metals, it puts the qualified-signal stream and the strategy's real performance next to your chat — not in a browser tab. A paid subscription is required; crypto (USDC) payment is supported via MoonPay.
